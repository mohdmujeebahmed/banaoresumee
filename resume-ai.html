Resume AI: Write a Better Resume with AI

Use Resume AI to write summaries, bullet points and cover letters, then edit them to sound like you.

AI can draft quickly, but you must add real facts and proof.

Build Your Resume Now

What AI does well

Rewrites bullets, suggests keywords, fixes grammar and tightens summaries.

What to avoid

Never invent jobs, numbers or skills. Recruiters check. Review with a resume review.

Workflow

Draft with AI, edit in the resume builder, pick a format, export.

Prompts to Use With Resume AI

  • "Rewrite this bullet to include a measurable result: [paste bullet]"
  • "Compare my resume with this job description and list missing keywords."
  • "Write a 3-line summary for a [role] with [X] years experience."

Rule: only keep numbers and claims that are true. You must be able to explain every line in an interview.

How to Write Strong Bullet Points (With Real Examples)

Formula: Action verb + what you did + tool or method + measurable result.

WeakStrong
Responsible for social media.Grew Instagram followers from 4,200 to 11,500 in 6 months using a Reels-first content calendar.
Worked on sales calls.Closed 38 B2B deals worth Rs 62 lakh in FY2025, exceeding target by 22%.
Helped with coding tasks.Built 12 REST APIs in Node.js, reducing page load time by 35%.
Handled customer queries.Resolved 60+ tickets daily with a 96% CSAT score.

Power verbs: Led, Built, Launched, Automated, Reduced, Increased, Negotiated, Designed, Analyzed, Optimized, Delivered, Streamlined. No numbers? Use scale: team size, users, budget, frequency.

Resume Summary Examples

Fresher: "B.Tech Computer Science graduate with hands-on experience in React and Node.js through 3 projects and a 3-month internship. Seeking a frontend developer role to build fast, accessible web apps."

Experienced: "Digital marketing manager with 7 years' experience in SEO and paid media. Grew organic traffic 220% for a SaaS brand and managed Rs 1.2 crore annual ad spend."

Career changer: "Former teacher moving into UX design. Completed Google UX Certificate, shipped 4 case studies and bring strong communication and user empathy."

Keep it to 2-3 lines, include your role, years or key skill, and one achievement.

10 Resume Mistakes That Cost Interviews

  1. Typos and grammar errors.
  2. One generic resume for every job.
  3. Duties instead of achievements.
  4. No numbers or results.
  5. Fancy graphics, tables and columns.
  6. Unprofessional email such as cooldude99@.
  7. Outdated or irrelevant jobs.
  8. Dense text with no white space.
  9. Lies or inflated titles.
  10. Poor file name. Use FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf.
