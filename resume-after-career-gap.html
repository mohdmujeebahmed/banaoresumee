Avoid these

What undermines trust

  • ✕Trying to hide the gap by removing all dates.
  • ✕Over-explaining or apologizing for the gap in detail.
  • ✕Not mentioning any upskilling or learning done during the break.
  • ✕Using a functional-only format that looks evasive.
FAQ

Common questions

Should I hide my career gap?

No — hiding it can damage trust once discovered. A brief, neutral explanation is more effective.

Will a gap hurt my chances?

Not if presented confidently — many recruiters are used to seeing career breaks.

Should I include freelance or volunteer work during the gap?

Yes — it shows initiative and strengthens the resume.
