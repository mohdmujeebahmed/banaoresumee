Resume Format: Best Formats and Examples for 2026

Choose the best resume format: reverse-chronological, functional or combination, with layout tips.

The format decides how easy your career story is to read.

Build Your Resume Now

Three formats

Reverse-chronological (best for most), functional (skills first) and combination (both). ATS handle chronological best.

Layout rules

One column, 10-12pt font, 0.5-1 inch margins, clear headings, no photos unless required.

Resume Summary Examples

Fresher: "B.Tech Computer Science graduate with hands-on experience in React and Node.js through 3 projects and a 3-month internship. Seeking a frontend developer role to build fast, accessible web apps."

Experienced: "Digital marketing manager with 7 years' experience in SEO and paid media. Grew organic traffic 220% for a SaaS brand and managed Rs 1.2 crore annual ad spend."

Career changer: "Former teacher moving into UX design. Completed Google UX Certificate, shipped 4 case studies and bring strong communication and user empathy."

Keep it to 2-3 lines, include your role, years or key skill, and one achievement.

How ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) Really Work

An ATS such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS or Taleo stores your resume, parses it into fields (name, jobs, dates, skills) and lets recruiters search by keywords. Most do not auto-reject you, but a resume that parses badly can be missed in search.

  • Use standard headings: Work Experience, Education, Skills, Projects.
  • Single-column layout. Multi-column designs, tables and text boxes can scramble reading order.
  • Put contact details in the body, not in the page header or footer, where some parsers skip them.
  • Match the job post's keywords honestly, for example "Python", "stakeholder management", "Google Analytics".
  • Spell out and abbreviate once: "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)".
  • Use a text-based PDF or DOCX. Never a scanned image or a PDF made from a screenshot.
  • Avoid icons, skill-rating bars and photos for key information.
