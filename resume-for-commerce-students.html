Entry 05 — Avoid these

What gets commerce resumes rejected

  • ✕Not naming specific accounting software (Tally, QuickBooks, SAP).
  • ✕Vague objective with no commerce specialization.
  • ✕Skipping GST/TDS keywords relevant to the role.
  • ✕No quantification of tasks handled.
FAQ

Common questions

What skills should commerce students highlight most?

Accounting software (Tally, QuickBooks), GST/TDS knowledge, and Excel — the most commonly ATS-scanned terms.

Do I need a certification to get hired?

Not mandatory, but Tally, GST, or Excel certifications meaningfully strengthen ATS match rate.

Should I include unpaid internships?

Yes — any relevant internship demonstrates practical exposure and should be included.
