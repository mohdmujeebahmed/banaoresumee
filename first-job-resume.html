Avoid these

What weakens a first resume

  • ✕Leaving the resume nearly empty instead of including activities.
  • ✕Using an unprofessional email address.
  • ✕Making the objective too vague or generic.
  • ✕Submitting as an image file instead of PDF/DOCX.
FAQ

Common questions

What if I've never had a job before?

Use school activities, projects, and volunteer work in place of a work experience section.

How long should it be?

One page is more than enough for a first-time applicant.

Should I include my age or date of birth?

No — this isn't required and isn't relevant to your qualifications.
