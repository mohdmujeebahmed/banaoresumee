The basics

What is a resume checker?

A resume checker analyzes your resume the way real hiring software does — flagging what's blocking you before a recruiter ever sees your application. Recruiters reportedly scan resumes in as little as 6–7 seconds, and a large share never make it past the ATS filter first.

Red flags

Common mistakes a checker catches

Multi-column layouts that scramble text order when parsed
Graphics, icons, and skill bars ATS software can't read
Missing keywords present in the job description
Inconsistent date formats across roles
Passive, duty-based bullets instead of quantified results
Non-standard section headers like "My Journey"
Typos, especially in the summary or most recent role
Resume length mismatched to seniority level
Weigh it up

Pros & cons of using a resume checker

✓ Pros

  • Instant, objective feedback instead of guessing
  • Catches formatting issues invisible to the eye
  • Quantifies keyword alignment with a specific job
  • Free tools make this accessible to everyone

✕ Cons

  • A high score doesn't guarantee an interview
  • Automated grammar checks can miss tone or nuance
  • Over-optimizing can feel robotic if unbalanced
  • Free tiers may limit checks per day
