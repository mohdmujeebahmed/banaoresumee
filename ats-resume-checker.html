what_is_this.exe

A free ATS resume checker scans your resume the same way an Applicant Tracking System does — parsing your file, comparing it to a job description, and scoring the match. Instead of guessing, you get an instant, itemized report of what to fix.

Good checkers evaluate: text parseability, keyword overlap, section structure, file format, and layout issues like tables or graphics that confuse parsers.

why_it_matters

Recruiters at mid-to-large companies often receive hundreds of applications per role. An ATS like Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo, or iCIMS pre-filters and ranks these resumes so recruiters only review the top matches. If your resume isn't ATS-friendly, it may never reach a human — regardless of your qualifications.

parse_sequence

  1. Parsing — text is extracted from your file into structured fields: name, contact info, experience, education, skills.
  2. Keyword Matching — your content is compared against the job description's required skills.
  3. Ranking — resumes are scored and sorted by match percentage.
  4. Recruiter Search — recruiters also query the resume database directly by keyword.

benefits.log

  • Catch parsing errors before a recruiter ever sees them
  • Identify missing keywords tied to the job description
  • Fix issues once instead of after silent rejections
  • Understand exactly why callbacks aren't coming
  • Improve interview call-back rates

run_scan()

  1. Upload your resume (PDF or DOCX)
  2. Paste the target job description
  3. Review your match score and flagged issues
  4. Fix formatting problems (tables, graphics, columns)
  5. Add missing keywords naturally
  6. Re-scan to confirm improvement
  7. Save in a standard, ATS-safe format

best_practices

  • Use standard headings: "Work Experience," "Education," "Skills"
  • Stick to a single-column layout
  • Use standard fonts — Arial, Calibri, Georgia
  • Save as .docx or a text-based PDF, never a scanned image
  • Avoid headers/footers for critical info — some parsers skip them
  • Spell out acronyms at least once

error_log

  • Graphics/icons for contact info or skill ratings — unreadable to ATS
  • Tables/columns — text often gets scrambled on parse
  • Headers/footers — contact details here are sometimes ignored
  • Unusual fonts — can render as garbled text
  • Wrong file types — image-based PDFs frequently fail
  • Missing keywords — a great resume still scores low without them

optimize()

  • Mirror language directly from the job description
  • Include a keyword-rich summary at the top
  • List technical skills in a dedicated section
  • Quantify achievements — numbers move rankings
  • Keep certifications and projects clearly labeled

expert_notes

Recruiters spend 6–8 seconds on an initial scan — even after ATS clears you, your top third needs to sell you fast. Don't over-optimize with keyword stuffing; both ATS and recruiters penalize unnatural repetition. Update your resume for every application — generic resumes rarely out-rank tailored ones.

pre_submit_checklist

  • Single-column layout
  • Standard section headings
  • Standard font, 10.5–12pt
  • Saved as .docx or text-based PDF
  • No tables, graphics, or icons for key info
  • Keywords matched to job description
  • Quantified achievements included
  • Contact info in main body, not header/footer
  • Spell-checked and proofread

faq_dump

What is an ATS resume checker?

A tool that scans your resume the way an ATS does, showing how well it will parse and match a job description.

Is a free checker accurate?

Good free checkers use the same parsing logic as popular ATS platforms, giving reliable directional feedback, though results vary slightly by vendor.

Do I need to sign up?

No — Banaoresumee's checker scans instantly without an account.

What file format should I use?

A .docx file or a text-based (non-scanned) PDF are the safest choices.

Do tables really break ATS parsing?

Yes, in many systems tables cause text to be read out of order or dropped entirely.

How many keywords should I include?

Enough to naturally cover the job description's core requirements — don't force keywords that don't fit.

Can graphics or a photo hurt my score?

Yes, most ATS software can't parse images, and some systems reject them outright.

Should I use the same resume for every job?

No — tailoring keywords per job description significantly improves your match score.

What's a good ATS match score?

Aim for 70% or higher against the job description's key requirements.

Do all companies use ATS?

Most mid-size and large companies do; smaller companies often review manually.

Can a cover letter affect ranking?

Some ATS platforms parse cover letters too — keep them keyword-relevant, though the resume carries more weight.

Will a creative design hurt my chances?

Heavily designed resumes with columns and graphics often score poorly on ATS, even if they look great to a human.

How often should I re-check my resume?

Every time you apply to a meaningfully different role or job description.

Can recruiters search resumes by keyword?

Yes — many ATS platforms let recruiters search their database directly, so relevance helps beyond active applications.

Is PDF or Word better for ATS?

A properly formatted, text-based PDF works with most modern ATS; Word (.docx) is the safest universal choice.
