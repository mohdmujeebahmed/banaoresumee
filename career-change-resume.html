Avoid these

What undermines a career change resume

  • ✕Keeping old job descriptions unchanged instead of reframing them.
  • ✕Not including a summary explaining the transition.
  • ✕Missing relevant certifications or courses for the new field.
  • ✕Using old-field jargon that doesn't match the new job posting.
FAQ

Common questions

Should I remove my old job titles?

No — keep them for honesty, but reframe the responsibilities using new-field language.

Do I need a certification to switch careers?

Not always required, but it significantly strengthens a career change resume.

What format is best for a career change?

A hybrid that leads with a summary and transferable skills, then an honest work history.
