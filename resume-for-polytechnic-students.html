What gets polytechnic resumes rejected

  • ✕Leaving out industrial or workshop training.
  • ✕Listing tools without saying how they were used.
  • ✕Scanned or image-based resume files.
  • ✕No quantified project outcomes.
FAQ

Common questions

Should I include industrial training as experience?

Yes — it's a core, expected part of a polytechnic resume and should be listed clearly with role and duration.

How many skills should I list?

8–10 relevant technical and soft skills, matched to the job posting.

Do I need a photo?

No — it's not required for most private-sector roles and can cause ATS parsing issues.
