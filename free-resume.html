How to Write Strong Bullet Points (With Real Examples)

Formula: Action verb + what you did + tool or method + measurable result.

WeakStrong
Responsible for social media.Grew Instagram followers from 4,200 to 11,500 in 6 months using a Reels-first content calendar.
Worked on sales calls.Closed 38 B2B deals worth Rs 62 lakh in FY2025, exceeding target by 22%.
Helped with coding tasks.Built 12 REST APIs in Node.js, reducing page load time by 35%.
Handled customer queries.Resolved 60+ tickets daily with a 96% CSAT score.

Power verbs: Led, Built, Launched, Automated, Reduced, Increased, Negotiated, Designed, Analyzed, Optimized, Delivered, Streamlined. No numbers? Use scale: team size, users, budget, frequency.

10 Resume Mistakes That Cost Interviews

  1. Typos and grammar errors.
  2. One generic resume for every job.
  3. Duties instead of achievements.
  4. No numbers or results.
  5. Fancy graphics, tables and columns.
  6. Unprofessional email such as cooldude99@.
  7. Outdated or irrelevant jobs.
  8. Dense text with no white space.
  9. Lies or inflated titles.
  10. Poor file name. Use FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf.
